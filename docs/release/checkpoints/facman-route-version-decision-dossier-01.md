# FacMan route/version decision dossier 01

Date: 12 August 2026

State: `recommendation_recorded_no_route_change`

## Decision recommendation

Retain Factorio 2.0.77 as the sole defined future execution-route candidate.
Retain Factorio 2.1.14 as the real archive/materialization corpus. Do not
silently substitute either identity for the other, and do not execute either
route under this WorkUnit.

This is the narrowest evidence-preserving decision. The 2.0.77 route already
has a frozen Windows x64, standalone non-Steam, menu, instance-isolated
selector and permit/observer law in
`release/index/successor_play_route.v2.toml`. The retained 2.1.14 evidence
proves bounded archive inspection and verification, but it explicitly did not
produce a complete staged tree, a session, or a launch verdict. Rebinding the
route to 2.1.14 would therefore discard a defined route without supplying
equivalent route evidence.

This recommendation grants no source-closure, qualification, execution,
Setup, route-capability, route-promotion, signing, or publication authority.
The route index and both route definitions remain unchanged.

## Evidence ledger

### 2.0.77 route

Present: exact selector, frozen policy, provider/observer and permit profiles,
and version-specific prior preflight evidence.

Absent: accepted source closure from the integrated task base; clean-host
candidate qualification; fresh stage, observer, baseline, and permit
identities; two independently permitted launches; complete technical packets;
human verdict; route capability; and promotion.

### 2.1.14 corpus

20,832-entry ZIP64/Deflate inspection and CRC verification; executable/module

Absent: a reviewed 2.1.14 execution policy and immutable route definition;
authenticated source binding in a route closure; complete staged installation;

two launches; human verdict; route capability; and promotion.

The 2.1.14 facts above are citations to the tracked checkpoint
`docs/release/checkpoints/facman-space-age-2.1.14-real-archive-acceptance-01.md`.
The licensed archive itself was not opened, copied, extracted, or modified by
this dossier.

## Exact reopening conditions

Reconsider a 2.1.14 route only through a separately reviewed immutable-input
transition that supplies all of the absent route evidence above and either
supersedes or explicitly retires the 2.0.77 selector. Reconsider execution of
the 2.0.77 route only after its source-closure and qualification prerequisites
are accepted on a capable clean Windows host. Managed 2.1.14 installation also
remains independently blocked on bounded ULK-independent Universal Setup
streaming/materialization, durable recovery, source trust, adoption authority,
and real-package fault/rollback qualification.

Until one of those paths is reviewed, the Technical Preview uses existing
installations, fake processes, and structured execution unavailability only.

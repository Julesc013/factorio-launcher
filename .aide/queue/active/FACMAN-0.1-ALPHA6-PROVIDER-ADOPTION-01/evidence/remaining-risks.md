# Remaining risks and retained boundaries

- Hosted artifacts are retention-limited; the closeout binds immutable run,
  job, source and digest identities rather than claiming permanent availability.
- The independent review inspected source and Git objects and did not rerun the
  heavy native/package matrix.
- The provider successor record intentionally preserves its pre-integration
  candidate state and must not be rewritten as a new source observation.
- Any provider revision, package byte, imported projection, license payload,
  staging identity or consumer-source change requires fresh qualification.
- Managed installation, real game execution, physical human experience,
  signing, tagging, publication, support, Alpha.6 completion, Beta.1 completion
  and stable-release authority remain outside this WorkUnit.

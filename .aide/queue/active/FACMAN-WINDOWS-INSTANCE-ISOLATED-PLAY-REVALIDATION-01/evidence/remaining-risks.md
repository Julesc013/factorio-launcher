# Remaining risks

The WorkUnit cannot complete autonomously because real Factorio execution and
the Play-to-menu observation require an explicit human-controlled evidence
session.

No prior observer baseline, permit, candidate packet, technical packet, or
human observation may be reused as current evidence. A new candidate must be
built from the bound accepted revisions, and all evidence must be collected
fresh.

Until a fresh operator verdict is `Pass`, product execution remains unavailable
and no route may be promoted. `Fail` and `Inconclusive` remain valid terminal
evidence outcomes for this WorkUnit but do not grant authority.

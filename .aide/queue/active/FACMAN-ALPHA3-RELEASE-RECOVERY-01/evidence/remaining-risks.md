  an honest record and must not be hidden by moving the tag.
- The draft remains private, unpublished, unsigned, unsupported, and subject
  to hash-bound human testing.

  already verified alpha.3 product bytes.

- Factorio execution, route, publication, signing, support, beta, RC, and
  stable authority remain absent.

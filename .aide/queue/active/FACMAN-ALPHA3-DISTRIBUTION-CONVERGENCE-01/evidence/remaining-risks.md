# Remaining risks

- The draft is private, unpublished, unsigned, and unsupported.
- Windows is the 0.1 support direction; macOS Intel and Ubuntu 24.04 remain experimental preview lanes.
- Apple Silicon, universal macOS, broad Linux compatibility, signing, and notarization are absent.
- Human portable/setup testing is pending on the exact downloaded hashes.
- No Factorio execution, route, publication, beta, RC, stable, signing, or support authority is granted.
- Any product-byte change requires a new forward-only prerelease version; the alpha.3 tag must not move.

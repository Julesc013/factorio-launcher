# Remaining risks

- FacMan retains its C++ direct, process, and daemon adapters until their
  product-neutral protocol implementation can be migrated without changing
  Factorio workspace selection or frontend behavior.
- The Windows instance-isolated Play candidate proof binds the previous ULK
  revision and must be regenerated before any human verdict or authority
  decision.
- No real Factorio process was started and no runtime, setup, network,
  credential, signing, or publication authority was granted.
- The Universal Launcher provider commit is local and not yet pushed or
  reviewed remotely.

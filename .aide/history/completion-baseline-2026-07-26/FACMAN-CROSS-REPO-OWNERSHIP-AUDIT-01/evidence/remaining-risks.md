# Ownership audit remaining risks

- Classification does not itself move code.
- Client, transport, platform, reference, transaction, preference, permit,
  and application-composition incubators remain in FacMan until their named
  extraction gates close.
- The Factorio workspace requires a split; moving it wholesale would leak
  product concepts into Universal Launcher.
- Permit extraction remains conditional on a second real provider consumer or
  an explicit retention decision.
- Cross-platform and hosted proof must be repeated after implementation moves.
- No runtime or release authority is inferred from a green ownership audit.

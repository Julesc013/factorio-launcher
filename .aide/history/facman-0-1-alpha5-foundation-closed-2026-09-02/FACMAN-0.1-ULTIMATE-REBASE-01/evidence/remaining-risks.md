# Remaining risks

- Windows desktop, install, accessibility, and real Factorio play acceptance
  still require a bound human verdict; this work does not fabricate one.
- macOS and Linux product stages pass contract qualification, but hosted native
  builds and platform-human acceptance remain future release gates.
- Signing, notarization, public publication, support promotion, release tagging,
  and real-play authority remain closed and outside this work unit.
- Alpha.4 is a comprehensive foundation candidate, not a beta or a claim that
  every later 0.2-1.0 feature has been pulled into 0.1. Content/world closure,
  managed installation, real Play/session closure, package-producer convergence,
  cross-platform semantic parity, migration, performance, and exact-byte human
  qualification remain separately tracked beta-readiness work.
- Any source change after artifact construction invalidates the recorded artifact
  hash and requires the package/verification step to be repeated.

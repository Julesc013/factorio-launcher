# Remaining risks

- The staged root is only an exact technical handoff. `prepare`, observer
  capture, permit issuance, Factorio execution and the human verdict remain
  separate and unperformed.
- Product execution authority and accepted public route count remain zero.
- The package is unsigned and unpublished and therefore is not a trusted
  distribution artifact.
- Any source, candidate, policy, Factorio or Instance identity change
  invalidates this qualification and requires a fresh remote-only rebuild.
